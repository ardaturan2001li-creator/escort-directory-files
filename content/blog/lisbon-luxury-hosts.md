## The Pinnacle of Portuguese Fine Dining

No luxury stay in Lisbon is complete without immersing oneself in the city’s vibrant haute cuisine landscape. Home to internationally celebrated Michelin-starred venues, Lisbon’s culinary masters reinterpret traditional Atlantic seafood and rich Iberian heritage with creative technical brilliance.

- **Belcanto**: Helmed by celebrated two-Michelin-starred Chef José Avillez, Belcanto in Chiado provides an extraordinary gastronomic voyage. Reserving the exclusive Chef’s Table in the heart of the kitchen offers front-row immersion into world-class culinary innovation.
- **Alma**: Chef Henrique Sá Pessoa delivers refined Portuguese cuisine defined by technical purity and emotional depth, nestled within an elegant, candlelit 18th-century setting in the heart of the historic center.
- **CURA**: Situated within the iconic Four Seasons Hotel Ritz Lisbon, CURA honors artistic culinary expression, turning regional artisanal ingredients into modern culinary masterpieces.
- **Fifty Seconds**: Suspended high above the Tagus River atop the Vasco da Gama Tower, this sky-high venue pairs sweeping coastal panoramas with an opulent multi-course tasting journey.
- **100 Maneiras**: Located in the bohemian district of Bairro Alto, Chef Ljubomir Stanisic curates bold, narrative-driven tasting menus that surprise and delight the most adventurous palates.

For international travelers, securing prime reservations at these highly sought-after restaurants requires planning weeks or months in advance, a service seamlessly orchestrated by experienced luxury concierge teams.
